# Warm-up content plan: @Solanites_ (2 weeks)

The goal is to build mystery before launch. Every post follows the hard rules: no contract address, buy or chart links, launch dates, or talk of price, returns or profit. Mythic and Secret designs never appear. Posts exist only as **Typefully drafts**: nothing is scheduled or published.

## Pillars

| Pillar | What it does | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Silhouette → reveal** | A blacked-out creature with a gold rim ("who's inside?"), then the full reveal a day later. Five creatures across the rarities shown: Mossling (Common), Frostkit (Common), Stormquill (Rare), Neonyx (Epic), Pyraurum (Legendary). | about every 2 days |
| **2. Lore drops** | Solgems, the four forms and the bloodlines, using copy from the game's own species lore. | 3 in 2 weeks |
| **3. "What will you hatch?" polls** | Replies and engagement, and they hint at the hidden bloodlines without confirming anything. | 4 in 2 weeks |
| **4. Behind the scenes** | Blender modelling and Remotion hatch clips. These drafts exist, but their media waits on Phases 2 and 5. | 3 in 2 weeks |
| **5. Vague countdowns** | "The eggs are warming", "did anyone else hear that?" No dates, ever. | 3 in 2 weeks |

## Notes for posting
- **Polls**: Typefully's API can't create X polls. Each poll draft lists its options in the text, and the draft's scratchpad says what to convert into a native X poll before posting.
- **Media pending**: the three BTS drafts carry a "MEDIA PENDING" note. Don't post them until the Blender or Remotion clip is attached.
- **3D upgrade**: once the Phase 2 Mossling render is approved, re-render the banner and the W-series silhouettes with 3D art and swap the media in the drafts.
- **Old campaign posts**: posts 04 and 07 on `marketing/x-campaign` show Mythic and Secret designs (Astravex, Nullborn, GENESIS). Hold them, because they break the new "no Mythic/Secret in X content" rule.
- **Copy source**: the copy text lives in `drafts.json`, generated by `build/drafts.py`. The images come from `build/render-warmup.mjs`.
