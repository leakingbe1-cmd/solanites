# Creature scenes: biomes and idle animation

Every Solanite is shown in its own painted world, behind its unchanged transparent artwork, and idles in a way its body can actually move. Owner brief of 20 Oct 2026; workstream "creature-biomes" (branch `feature/creature-biomes`).

## How it works

- **Config:** `src/lib/creatureScenes.ts` holds, per public species id, its **biome** (id, name, why it belongs, fallback tones, environmental particle, readability `lift`/`calm`), its **anatomy** (how it moves, which parts it has, whether it has anatomical light), and its **idle profile** for each of the four stages.
- **One plate per species, framed per stage.** A single painted plate (`public/scenes/<id>/plate.webp`, 1024²) serves all four stages. `STAGE_FRAMING` frames it: the Eggling sees a close, dim, softly out-of-focus corner of its world; each stage opens it a little more; the Ascended sees all of it, fully lit, with its light gently stirring. No second image per stage, so the world never changes identity between stages.
- **Rendering:** `CreatureScene` (biome + light + particles + the creature) wraps `CreatureArt`, whose new optional `idle` prop renders `IdleArt` over the painted art. Sizes: `card` (Collection, tiles, Hatchery cards: body idle only, one layer), `quiet` (homepage Market Bond plate), `feature` (showcases, hatch reveal) and `detail` (creature profile).
- **Idle:** the whole body moves on nested layers with unrelated periods (no exact loop), with motions chosen by how the species moves (breathe, stalk, hover, swim, glide, pulse, coil, rock). At feature and detail sizes, **parts** move on their own: a region of the creature's own artwork is cut out by a polygon and turned about a hinge on the body (`sway`), or sheared from its attachment line (`skew`, `bend`), or eased toward it (`fold`). Glow maps (`glow-<n>.webp`) pulse only anatomical light, derived offline from the shipped art, within canon C5's rarity limits.
- **Performance:** transforms and opacity only; one shared IntersectionObserver pauses scenes off screen; cards run one layer and no light stir; particles only at the two large sizes.
- **Reduced motion:** all scene animation and particles stop; biomes still show.
- **Fallbacks:** an unknown or unrevealed species, or a sealed slot, gets the generic CSS-only **sealed vault**; a plate that fails to load leaves the biome's gradient.

## Canon

The owner's brief approves painted environments behind the transparent art, and sparse environmental particles. Canon W1/W2 stay in force: plates are composed with no floor, horizon, pedestal, halo, rays, sparkles or lens flare (the lower edge dissolves into mist, water or cloud); no glow shape is drawn behind a creature; `lift`/`calm` only adjust a whole plate's exposure or saturation; depth-of-field blur stays below 10 px (the site QA treats 40 px as a glow blob). Creature art is never edited.

## Classified (Mythic and Secret)

No classified species is named, given a biome, or given motion anywhere in the repository or the client. Their slots render the sealed vault, still. A disclosed bundle (`ClassifiedBundle`) may carry an optional `scene` (`{ scene, assets }`), which the browser registers with `registerScene`; it is meant to come from the private Blob store, with plate and glow URLs served after the existing access checks. Until the store is connected, revealed classified species show the sealed vault.

## Performance note

**Method.** Local production builds served side by side: baseline `design/eclipse-vigil-code` (4d42aae, before the scenes) on :3200 and this branch on :3100, measured with headless Chromium 1194 (Playwright). Frame rate: `requestAnimationFrame` count over 3 s with a screen of Collection scenes idling, 3 alternating runs each. Weight: bytes on the wire per page (CDP `Network.loadingFinished`), 390 × 844 at 2× DPR, mobile emulation.

| Collection, idling | Baseline | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop 1440 × 900 | 17.1 fps (18/16/17) | 18.4 fps (17/19/19) |
| Phone 390, 4× CPU throttle | 58.1 fps | 60.4 fps |

**Caveat.** The container has no GPU, so Chromium composites in software: the desktop figure is low for the whole site with or without scenes (every animation on the page disabled: 60 fps), and run-to-run noise is about ±3 fps. Within that noise the scenes cost nothing measurable; the in-page check in `scripts/qa/scenes-site.mjs` (same page, scene animation on vs off) has passed and failed on consecutive runs for that reason. Real-device GPU measurement is still to do. Everything scenes animate is a transform or opacity on composited layers; off-screen scenes are paused; cards run one layer.
