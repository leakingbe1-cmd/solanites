## Verdicts

### Eclipse Phoenix: **REFINE**

- **Works:** hand-painted finish matches C1; three-quarter view facing left; asymmetrical wings; deep violet-blue darkness with a warm key from the upper left and a violet rim on the right; one ember Solgem on the chest as the only strong emission; no lightning, flare or sparkles; native 2752 × 1536.
- **Fails P2:** it reads as a golden **eagle**: no tail plumes, no crest, no feathers turning to light. Nothing says phoenix.
- **Fails P3:** the eclipse sits directly behind the head and reads as a halo.
- **Fails P4:** the near wing fills the top third of the headline zone (16.3% mean luminance against < 8%).
- Not monumental yet: the figure is cropped by the lower edge and has little scale cue.
- **Keep:** the finish, palette, lighting, chest Solgem and the eclipse motif. **Change:** phoenix anatomy, eclipse placement, a clear left 40%.

### Velynx: **REJECT**

- **Fails identity (C8):** on all fours. The brief and canon require an upright biped. This is the second independent generation (after Creature Studio's V-A) that read "upright digitigrade" as a quadruped, so the wording, not luck, is the problem.
- Mechanical **joint rings** at elbows and knees (C5) make it read as a robot; **ear notches** drawn as punched holes rather than a split tip; **eyeliner and lashes** (C11); no cheek tufts; paws instead of long fingers.
- **C9 fails** narrowly (69 / 48): the charcoal body sinks into the background at 48 px.
- **Works:** the sly face, the S-curved tail with a teal leaf tip, glyphs built from lines and dots (no letters), the glyph tile under one forepaw, the chest gem.
- Kept on file as a record only. Not a pose, anatomy or rendering reference (V-A already serves as the rendering reference). Any next Velynx uses the mandatory wording in Creature Studio's reconciliation report §3.

### Mossling: **REFINE** (strongest of the four)

- **Works:** the agreed body plan (C8, decision 1): low broad grazer, moss bed with leaf shingles that read as plant, not shell, full pale-wood antlers, bark-textured shins, blunt hoof-toes, jointed legs, short leafy tail. The heavy-lidded, contented face is the best face in the round (C11). C9 passes comfortably (97 / 76). Clearly better than the shipped vector, whose stages are one shape.
- **Fails palette (C8):** body hue 45° (olive-khaki skin) against the species body `#3fbf7f` (150°, mint green). The painted reference strip is also a lighter blue-green. The body should be moss-green, not tan.
- The far antler roots in the back moss rather than the skull.
- Eight spores, some more than a body-width away (C5 allows a few close to the body).
- The forehead Solgem glows orange; the shipped gem uses the species accent `#14f195` (green), so it is off-palette.
- **Keep:** body plan, shingles, antlers, face, materials. **Change:** palette, gem colour, antler root, fewer spores.

### Cryovant: **REFINE**

- **Works:** a strong, ownable silhouette (faceted crown over a frosted skirt, a curtain of graduated diamond-shard chains, two ribbon arms); convincing ice and crystal materials with spectral colour on only a few edges; a refracted core gem; C9 passes (127 / 60). Clearly distinct from the shipped wireframe dome.
- **Fails C5:** an orange bloom in the air at upper left and a cyan bloom at right; both register as part of the creature in the 48 px black-fill.
- **Expression:** the narrowed photophores read as **angry**, not the brief's "cool appraisal".
- **Rim:** cyan rather than the canon's restrained violet (the prompt used the superseded art-bible wording).
- The crown and chains lean toward jewellery and chandelier, which the brief reserves for Ascended; Evolved should be one step simpler.
- **Keep:** silhouette, materials, shard chains, core gem. **Change:** no air glow, calm photophores, violet rim, a simpler Evolved crown.

## Prompt lessons (for whoever generates next)

1. Lighting text in all four prompts said "cool cyan-violet rim"; the canon now says restrained violet `#7d5cf0`.
2. "Upright digitigrade" is not enough for Velynx (two failures). Use the reconciliation report's explicit stance wording and a pose reference image.
3. Name the palette with hex values from `species.ts`; the model drifted Mossling to khaki without them.
4. Add "no coloured glow in the background around the creature" to the negatives.
