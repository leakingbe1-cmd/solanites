# Biome plate prompts

One painted environment plate per public species, generated once and reused for all four stages (see `docs/creature-scenes.md`). Every prompt is the **style block** followed by the species' **scene**. Classified species are not listed here: their scenes are private (server-only, pending the private Blob store).

Model and size: `nano-banana-2.1`, 1K, 1:1, text only (no reference image, so no creature anatomy can leak into a scene). Every task is logged in `generation-ledger.md`.

## Style block (all plates)

> Hand-painted 2D fantasy environment background for a premium creature-collection game, a painterly illustration with visible but controlled brushwork, soft painted edges and three-value painted volume; not a 3D render, not photoreal, not flat vector. Square composition at eye level. This is an EMPTY environment plate: the centre of the image is open, calm, softly hazed space with low contrast and no objects, reserved for a creature that will be added later; all environmental detail sits toward the left and right edges and in the far background, with atmospheric depth. Lighting: a warm gold key light (#FFE2A8) from the upper left and a restrained violet (#7D5CF0) ambient light from behind, deep low-key shadows; the whole plate is dark and muted so a brightly painted creature placed in front stands out. The lower edge dissolves into soft mist, water or cloud: no floor plane in the middle, no horizon line, no pedestal or platform, no cast shadows on a floor. No sun disc, no light rays or god rays, no halo or glowing ring, no lens flare, no sparkle stars. No animals, creatures, people, figures or characters of any kind, no text, letters, logos, frames or borders.
