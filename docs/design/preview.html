Sigil

The Ascending Egg.

  • One metal. Gold throughout; the gem is the only colour.
  • The halo is lifted straight from the hero phoenix, so mark and key art match.
  • Four feathers a side, bowed on the leading edge, so they read as wings rather than rays.
  • Twelve ticks on the ring: also the incubation progress ring and the loader.
  • Three versions: full (48px and up), emblem (24px and up), glyph (favicon).
