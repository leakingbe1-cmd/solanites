# Round 1 concepts: Velynx, Mossling, Cryovant

A small first round to test whether the art bible produces **three genuinely different but cohesive characters** before any evolution lines are made. Proposed only; nothing is generated until the spend in [`../generation-options.md`](../generation-options.md) is approved.

## Scope

- **Evolved stage only**: the species' canonical form, with mature anatomy, the full face and the signature features.
- **Two directions per species** (A and B), so the review compares a real choice rather than accepting or rejecting a single image.
- **6 images**, plus up to 3 retries for broken anatomy, so **≤ 9**.
- Same model, size, background, camera and light for all six, so differences come from design, not settings.

## What Round 1 tests

| Test | How it is judged |
|---|---|
| Distinctive faces | each face has a readable expression (brow or lid + eye + mouth), and the three faces differ in eye type: slit almond (Velynx), heavy-lidded round (Mossling), photophore ring (Cryovant) |
| Memorable silhouettes | black-fill at 64 px: tall zig-zag, low dome with antlers, gem-cut dome over a curtain |
| Believable anatomy | joints, weight and load paths; no tubes, no extra limbs |
| Tactile materials | matte fur vs moss and leaf vs ice-glass, each recognisable with colour removed (greyscale check) |
| Element contrast | Shadow / Verdant / Crystal read apart in shape, material and light, not just hue |
| Cohesion | the three side by side look like one game: same light, line, value structure and finish |

## The six concepts

Every prompt starts with the shared style block (below). References: the species' Evolved baseline render (body plan and identity), plus `velynx-inspiration.png` for Velynx palette only.

### Velynx (Legendary, Shadow)

- **V-A · Fence-walker** (the brief as written in `velynx.md`): upright digitigrade serval-lemur build, notched quill-nib ears, weight on one leg, one paw on a glyph tile, amused one-sided smile.
- **V-B · Glyph-scribe**: the same species pushed further from the "upright psychic cat" archetype. A lower, crouched three-point stance on the glyph tile, longer lemur-like fingers tracing a glyph in the air, the tail curling overhead into a question mark. Tests whether a less human posture is more ownable.

### Mossling (Common, Verdant) · also its canon review (decision 1)

- **M-A · Grazer** (shipped body plan, as briefed in `mossling.md`): low broad dome, moss bed with roof-tile leaf shingles, long soft muzzle, heavy-lidded contented face, full pale-wood antlers.
- **M-B · Moss fawn** (tests the painted sheet): lighter, taller, deer-like legs and a bigger-eyed head, still with the moss bed and antlers. Shown next to M-A so the canon decision can be made from images.

### Cryovant (Rare, Crystal)

- **C-A · Gem-cut bell** (as briefed in `cryovant.md`): wide gemstone-crown bell over a frosted skirt, a ring of five photophores, graduated shard curtain, refracted core gem.
- **C-B · Lantern**: a taller, narrower bell like a hanging lantern, a tighter vertical shard curtain, and the photophores arranged as two "eyes" plus a smaller row, giving a stronger face. Tests whether more vertical height separates it from Glimmeray and Pallow in the grid.

## Shared style block (Round 1)

> Hand-painted 2D creature concept illustration for a premium creature-collecting game. Clean, graphic silhouette; coloured linework in the darkest local colour (never black) that thins where the light hits; soft painted volume in three values with subtle, controlled brush texture. Key light from the upper left, cool cyan-violet rim light from the right. Three-quarter view facing left, camera at the creature's eye level. Full body centred, filling about 80% of the square frame, on a plain flat near-black background (#07060d). No ground, no halo, no light rays, no sparkles, no particles beyond a few close to the body, no text, no logo, no emblem, no frame.
>
> Avoid: 3D render, plastic or glossy vector look, anime, chibi mascot, extra or fused limbs, pseudo-letters, jewellery, armour straps, resemblance to existing franchise creatures.

## Per-concept prompts

Append to the style block.

**V-A.** A slender, upright, digitigrade feline creature standing on its toes with its weight on one leg, built like a serval crossed with a lemur: deep chest, narrow waist, long forearms held close with long fingers. Very long swept-back ears with a notched second point two-thirds up and deep-teal insides; backward-sweeping cheek tufts. Matte charcoal-navy fur with a satin sheen on the shoulders, teal paw pads. Half-lidded violet eyes ringed in turquoise with slit pupils, head tilted, a sly one-sided closed-mouth smile. Thin cyan glyph markings made of right angles, short arcs and dots follow the sternum, shoulders, forearms and shins and ring the tail. A high S-curved tail ending in a teal leaf-shaped tip. One paw rests on a small floating cyan glyph tile. A small diamond gem on its chest.

**V-B.** The same slender charcoal-navy feline creature in a low crouched three-point stance on a small floating cyan glyph tile, one long-fingered hand raised, tracing a glowing cyan glyph in the air. Very long swept-back notched ears with deep-teal insides, half-lidded violet eyes ringed in turquoise, a curious, mischievous expression. Cyan right-angle glyph markings along its limbs and spine. The long tail curls high over its back into a question-mark shape ending in a teal leaf tip. A small diamond gem on its chest.

**M-A.** A low, broad, domed four-legged grazer walking slowly forward with its head held low. A long soft muzzle with a rounded nose pad, leaf-shaped ears pointing sideways, heavy-lidded content dark-green eyes and a soft closed smile. Its back is a thick bed of moss with rows of overlapping leaf shingles like roof tiles (not a shell). Full branching pale-wood antlers with fine grain and small leaves. Short legs with bark-textured shins and three blunt hoof-toes, a short leafy tail. Blue-green moss, pale sage belly and muzzle. A few tiny glowing spores close to its back. A small diamond gem half-buried in the moss on its forehead.

**M-B.** A small, light-footed mossy fawn-like creature standing alert on slender deer-like legs with small hooves, a rounded body covered in a bed of blue-green moss with overlapping leaf shingles along its back. A big rounded head with large curious dark-green eyes, a short soft muzzle, leaf-shaped ears and short branching pale-wood antlers with small leaves. A gentle, shy expression. A small diamond gem in the moss on its forehead. A few tiny glowing spores close to its back.

**C-A.** A floating crystal jellyfish whose bell is cut like a gemstone: a sharp faceted crown over a soft, frosted blue-white skirt with tiny icicle points. A ring of five small glowing photophores under the rim, the front two slightly narrowed in cool appraisal. Many long chains of graduated diamond shards (large near the bell, tiny at the tips) hang in a curtain, with two smooth translucent ribbon arms in the centre. A bright diamond gem core visible inside the clear blue bell, refracted into two faint copies. Spectral colour only on two or three facet edges.

**C-B.** A floating crystal jellyfish with a tall, narrow bell like a hanging lantern: clear blue faceted ice above a frosted skirt. Two larger glowing photophores under the rim act as calm, appraising eyes, with a row of tiny lights between them. A tight vertical curtain of graduated diamond-shard chains and two ribbon arms. A bright diamond gem core inside the bell. Cold, precise and elegant. No rainbow rays.

## After generation

1. Save each image to `art/creature-studio/round-1/<concept>.png` (review folder, not read by the site) and record model, prompt, task ID and credit cost in `asset-map.json`.
2. Build a comparison page: each species' original Evolved stage beside its A and B concepts, then the three chosen concepts side by side for the cohesion check, with black-fill and greyscale strips.
3. You pick A or B (or a mix) per species. Only then: the four-stage lines and the 12-stage comparison page.
