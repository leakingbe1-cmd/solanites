# Solanites creature design system

## 1. Audit of the original collection

All 24 species were drawn from **four shared body templates** (`drake`, `serpent`, `manta`, `phoenix`), and **every template shared the same head**. The only differences between species were colour, a choice of five horn shapes, and a small marking motif.

| Repeated trait | Where it appeared |
|---|---|
| Identical front-facing head: rounded skull, broad muzzle, cheek spikes, almond eyes, angry brow | All 24 species |
| Same pear-shaped torso, belly plate, stubby forelegs, two haunches and spade tail | 7 drakes (Mossling, Frostkit, Duskwing, Emberlisk, Neonyx, Aurelion, Astravex, plus GENESIS) |
| Same bat wing, mirrored | Every drake |
| Same S-shaped tube body with whiskers and fin mane | 6 serpents (Driftfin, Sproutwyrm, Vael, Zephrax, Solvanth, Orbelith) |
| Same diamond wing, core gem and tail | 5 mantas (Glimmeray, Cryovant, Aurorath, Aurelith, Eventide) |
| Same fan of feathers around a drake torso | 5 phoenixes (Pallow, Stormquill, Prismara, Pyraurum, NULLBORN) |
| Same pose: front-facing, centred, sitting | All 24 |

**Result:** 24 colourways of 4 characters. Legendary and Mythic were told apart only by gold or platinum colour and aura particles.

## 2. What stays shared (the "same universe" rules)

These are kept so every species still belongs to the same game:

- **Lighting:** the key light comes from the top left. Each form uses a radial body gradient (light → body → shadow) with a soft dark rim line and one or two white specular highlights.
- **Materials:** smooth stylised forms; translucent fins and membranes (`fin` gradient); luminous accents drawn with the shared `glow` filter.
- **The Solgem:** every Solanite carries exactly one diamond Solgem. Where it sits depends on the species (forehead, chest, back, core, lure or crown).
- **Eyes:** all eyes use the same eye gradient (white core, eye colour, accent rim) plus a specular dot. Shape and count vary per species: almond, round, slit, glowing orb, compound, single eye or visor.
- **Presentation:** a 400×400 stage, a ground shadow (smaller for floating species), the rarity aura behind, and the same idle bob. Each body plan fits the frame without cropping.
- **Evolution:** Hatchling → Apex grows the anatomy (antlers branch, fins lengthen, quills multiply), not just the size. The Apex form also gains a halo.

## 4. How rarity shows in the design, not just colour

- **Common:** one clear idea and a simple silhouette (dome, eel, orb, ray, worm, fox).
- **Rare:** an unusual structure (rising neck, stilt legs, four wings, a body made of glass, armour plates).
- **Epic:** an elaborate silhouette with a built-in light source (lure, aurora curtains, ring coil, shard fan).
- **Legendary:** large, ornate compositions that fill the frame (a rearing dragon, a coil around a sun, flight, a gilded shell).
- **Mythic:** cosmic anatomy, with star-fields *inside* the body and celestial parts (antlers, planetary rings, an eclipse).
- **Secret:** breaks the rules. Abstract or inverted forms that do not look like any other species.
